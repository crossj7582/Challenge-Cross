JANUARY 1942 — PACIFIC THEATER

Pearl Harbor has changed naval warfare in the Pacific. Aircraft carriers, reconnaissance, aircraft operations, surface forces, and logistics will determine whether a task force can continue to fight.

This educational historical simulation places two commanders in charge of comparable 1942 carrier task forces. Each commander must make decisions with incomplete information and manage the consequences of those decisions.

AIR POWER

F4F Wildcats, SBD Dauntlesses, and TBD Devastators.

SURFACE FORCE

Carrier, light carrier, battleship, cruisers and destroyers.

RECONNAISSANCE

SOC Seagull observation aircraft provide limited intelligence.

COMMAND

Protect combat power while creating opportunities to damage the opposing force.
