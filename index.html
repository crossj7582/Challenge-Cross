Commander Briefing

THE MISSION

It is 1942. Carrier aviation has become the decisive arm of Pacific naval warfare. Each commander controls a U.S. carrier task force.

Your decisions affect fuel, aircraft, ship condition, position, and the strength of future strikes.

There is no reset between rounds. Damaged and destroyed assets remain damaged or destroyed.

COMMAND PRINCIPLES

  • Protect the carrier.
  • Use reconnaissance and intelligence before committing scarce aircraft.
  • Balance striking power with defensive coverage.
  • Remember that every aircraft launched is a sortie commitment.
  • Surface ships, aircraft, fuel, and repair capacity all matter.
