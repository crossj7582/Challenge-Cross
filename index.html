PACIFIC THEATRE WAR GAME

Mission Briefing

It is 1942. Two American carrier strike groups are operating in the Pacific. Each commander must maneuver ships, aircraft, reconnaissance, defense screens, and strike forces through fifteen rounds of combat.

Every decision changes the battlefield. Ships and aircraft committed or destroyed during the battle affect the force available in later rounds.

Command carefully. Protect your carrier, manage your aircraft, and destroy the opposing strike force.

Commander B

The map will display the individual ships in your task force. Each ship type has a different operational role.

  • Aircraft Carrier (CV) — launches fighters, dive bombers and torpedo bombers.
  • Light Carrier (CVL) — additional aircraft/CAP.
  • Fast Battleship (BB) — heavy guns and AA.
  • Heavy Cruiser (CA) — long-range guns, AA and scouting.
  • Light Cruiser (CL) — AA, screening and command/support.
  • Destroyer (DD) — carrier screen, ASW, AA and surface defense.
  • Fleet Oiler (AO) — refueling.
  • Destroyer Escort / Escort — specialized protection.
  • Repair/Support Ship — repair, ammunition and supplies.
