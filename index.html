THE PACIFIC HAS CHANGED
December 1941 — Emergency Fleet Command Briefing

FROM: ADMIRAL CHESTER W. NIMITZ

"Pearl Harbor has been attacked. The Pacific Fleet has been forced into a new kind of war."

The battleship will no longer be the only weapon around which naval power is organized.

Aircraft carriers can strike hundreds of miles beyond the range of surface guns.

The commander who can find the enemy first, launch the correct aircraft, protect his carrier, and recover his force will control the battle.

C. W. Nimitz

YOUR ORDERS

The United States Navy needs a new generation of carrier strike commanders.

You will command a carrier task force against another student commander.

  • Find the enemy.
  • Interpret intelligence.
  • Select the correct aircraft.
  • Protect your carrier.
  • Build your formation.
  • Select your target.
  • Launch your strike.
  • Recover your aircraft.
  • Keep your force combat effective.

COMMAND MANUAL

You are not expected to memorize everything.

Your course notes are your COMMAND MANUAL.

Every round will require you to apply something you have learned.

  • Carrier anatomy
  • Pacific geography
  • Aircraft capabilities
  • Reconnaissance
  • Carrier formations
  • Strike sequence
  • CAP
  • Flight deck operations
  • Target selection
  • Damage control

TOURNAMENT

Two commanders enter each battle.

Each battle contains 10 different operational scenarios.

Each commander makes a decision every round.

The winner advances.

The loser is eliminated.

Scenarios already used in the tournament are protected from unnecessary repetition.
