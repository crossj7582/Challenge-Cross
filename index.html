COMMAND BRIEF

January 1942.

The United States has entered the Pacific War. Aircraft carriers are becoming the centerpiece of naval warfare. Your task force must operate aircraft, ships, reconnaissance, defense, maneuver, and strike operations as one fighting force.

MISSION STANDARD

Protect the carrier. Preserve combat power. Use reconnaissance. Make decisions before the enemy makes them for you.

This is a command simulation. Your decisions change the battlefield. Destroyed ships and aircraft remain destroyed.

MISSION FORMAT

15 ROUNDS

Commander A acts first.

The device is then passed to Commander B.

Commander B sees the changed battlefield and responds.

Only after both commanders act is the round resolved.

The battle continues until one force loses its carrier or the 15-round campaign ends.
