TACTICAL PRINCIPLE

Your task is not to find a quiz answer. You must manage aircraft, ships, fuel, repairs, reconnaissance and defensive formations while adapting to an opponent who is making decisions at the same time.

COMMAND BRIEFING

Pacific Carrier Operations

YOUR MISSION

Command your carrier task force through a 15-round engagement.

Your opponent is another American carrier task force of comparable strength. You must use the information available to you, make tactical decisions and adapt to battlefield changes.

You will not receive points for individual decisions. The battlefield changes according to what both commanders do.

INITIAL FORCE

1 Aircraft Carrier (CV)
3 Heavy Cruisers (CA)
4 Destroyers (DD)
18 Fighters
18 Dive Bombers
12 Torpedo Bombers

IMPORTANT

Aircraft destroyed in combat are permanently removed from your available air group.

Ships destroyed in combat are permanently removed from the task force.

Damage to your carrier can persist from one round to the next.

When you launch a strike, you decide the exact number and type of aircraft committed.
