COMMAND BRIEF

The Pacific War has changed the nature of naval warfare. Aircraft carriers, aircraft, cruisers, battleships, and destroyers must operate together as a single fighting force.

You will command an American carrier task force through fifteen tactical rounds.

Every decision changes the battlefield. Ships can be damaged or destroyed. Aircraft can be lost. Your force carries its condition from one round to the next.

There are no perfect decisions. Your objective is to preserve your carrier, protect your striking power, and destroy the opposing force.
