Rules

  • Both commanders act every round.
  • Commander A acts first.

  • The round report appears only after both commanders have acted.
  • Destroyed aircraft remain destroyed.
  • Destroyed ships remain destroyed.
  • Carrier damage carries into later rounds.
  • There are no quiz points.
