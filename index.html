PACIFIC THEATRE WAR GAME

Mission Briefing

It is 1942. Two American carrier strike groups are operating in the Pacific.

Each commander must maneuver ships, aircraft, reconnaissance forces and defensive screens through fifteen rounds of combat.

Ships and aircraft lost during the battle remain lost. Every decision changes the battlefield.
