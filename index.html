Commander's Mission

You are commanding an American carrier task force in the Pacific during 1942.

You will face another American carrier task force commanded by your opponent. Both commanders begin with comparable forces.

This is not a quiz. There are no memorized answers. Every decision changes the battlefield.

You will have 15 rounds to locate, maneuver, protect, strike, repair, and destroy the opposing force.

Rules of Engagement

  • Both commanders act every round.
  • Commander A acts first.
  • The device then passes to Commander B.
  • Commander B sees the changed battlefield before acting.
  • Ships and aircraft lost in combat remain lost.
  • Damaged ships carry their damage into later rounds.
  • Aircraft committed to strikes are no longer available until recovered.
  • There is no reset between rounds.
  • The final battle condition determines the winner.
