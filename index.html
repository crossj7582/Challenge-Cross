Commander's Mission

You are commanding an American carrier task force operating in the Pacific during 1942.

You will face another American carrier task force commanded by your opponent.

Both commanders begin with comparable forces.

This is a tactical command exercise. There are no memorized answers. Every decision changes the battlefield.

You will have 15 rounds to locate, maneuver, protect, strike, repair and defeat the opposing task force.

Rules Of Engagement

  • Both commanders act every round.
  • Commander A acts first.
  • The device then passes to Commander B.
  • Commander B sees the changed battlefield.
  • Ships destroyed remain destroyed.
  • Ship damage carries into later rounds.
  • Aircraft losses are permanent.
  • There is no reset between rounds.
  • The final operational condition determines the winner.
