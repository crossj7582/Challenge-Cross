Fleet Command Briefing

YOUR MISSION

You are a U.S. Navy carrier strike commander in training. You will command a carrier task force through a series of ten operational challenges.

Your success depends on your ability to apply your command notes, not simply guess.

Every decision can gain or lose Victory Points, aircraft, readiness, fuel, and intelligence.

TOURNAMENT RULE

This is a head-to-head tournament.

Commander A and Commander B will each make decisions for ten rounds.

The winner advances. The defeated commander is eliminated.

A scenario used in an earlier tournament battle will not be intentionally reused while unused scenarios remain.

YOUR COMMAND MANUAL

  • Carrier anatomy
  • Strike sequence
  • Aircraft employment
  • Reconnaissance
  • Carrier formations
  • Target selection
  • CAP / fighter defense
  • Flight-deck operations
  • Recovery procedures
  • Pacific geography

Your notes are your command manual.

SCORING

Each commander begins each battle with:

  • 10 Victory Points
  • 100 Aircraft Readiness
  • 100 Fuel
  • 100 Intelligence

Correct command decisions improve your force. Poor decisions can reduce your resources.
