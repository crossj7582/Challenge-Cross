MISSION BRIEFING — 1942

Two American carrier strike-force commanders have been assigned to the same operational theatre. Each commander must maneuver, screen, search, launch sorties, defend the carrier, and preserve enough combat power to survive fifteen rounds of naval warfare.

Your decisions will permanently change the battlefield. Ships that are destroyed disappear from the tactical map. Aircraft lost during sorties are removed from your available strength. Damaged units remain damaged until repaired.

COMMAND PRINCIPLE: Your aircraft carrier is the center of your strike force. If its strength reaches 0%, your command ends immediately.

1942 — CARRIER WARFARE

COMMANDER'S BRIEFING

The aircraft carrier has changed naval warfare. Your task force combines carriers, battleships, cruisers, destroyers, aircraft, fuel, repair capability, and reconnaissance.

You will fight for 15 rounds. Each round gives one commander an opportunity to move ships, establish a defensive perimeter, conduct reconnaissance, launch a sortie, or prepare for the next engagement.

Your opponent will then see the changed battlefield before making their decision.

Aircraft that return undamaged from a sortie return to the operational aircraft tally. Damaged aircraft remain unavailable until repaired. Destroyed aircraft are permanently lost.

Ships work the same way. A damaged ship remains on the map. A ship reduced to 0% is destroyed and disappears permanently.
