EDUCATIONAL HISTORY RESOURCE — U.S. HISTORY / WORLD WAR II

EDUCATIONAL PURPOSE

This interactive historical simulation places students in the role of a U.S. Navy carrier task-force commander during the Pacific War of 1942.

Students apply historical knowledge of aircraft carriers, naval aviation, reconnaissance, defensive screens, surface combat, logistics, damage control, and tactical decision-making.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

  • Analyze the role of aircraft carriers in Pacific naval warfare.
  • Apply historical naval terminology and tactics.
  • Evaluate competing tactical decisions and consequences.
  • Understand the importance of reconnaissance and intelligence.
  • Analyze logistics, damage control, and force preservation.
  • Explain how individual decisions affected larger military operations.

HISTORICAL NOTE

The simulation uses a simplified classroom model of 1942 carrier warfare. Its purpose is to help students analyze historical strategy and consequences rather than reproduce every technical detail of naval combat.
