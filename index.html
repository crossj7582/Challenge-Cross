PACIFIC THEATRE WAR GAME

Mission Briefing

It is 1942. Two American carrier strike groups are operating in the Pacific. Each commander must maneuver ships, aircraft, reconnaissance, defense screens and strike forces through fifteen rounds of combat.

Every decision changes the battlefield. Ships and aircraft destroyed or committed during the battle remain unavailable in later rounds.

Protect your carrier, manage your aircraft and destroy the opposing strike force.
